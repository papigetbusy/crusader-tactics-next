# Legendary rework: proposal for the owner (2026-10-17)

**Approved and built (2026-10-17).** The owner's answers: pictures from Grok's sheets (Warrior Sword 3, Tank Axe 1, Knight Lance 3, Dragon Knight Lance 2, Ranger Bow 1, Gunslinger Gun 2, Assassin Dagger 1, Mage Wand 3, Healer Staff 1, Witch Staff 3); class powers OK; epic sets OK; no new ordinary weapons. The weapon names follow the pictures: Bloodcrescent, Voidreaver, Frostspire, Dragonbone Lance, Silverwing, Cinderhowl, Nightfang, Umbral Scepter, Staff of Eternity, Hexflame Crook. Built as written, with these small changes:
- The charms already had 3 bonuses, so they (and the legendary armor) went up to 5, like every legendary.
- Arcane Echo: 20% chance a spell hits each target a second time (the same as casting it twice, without a second cooldown).
- Shadowstep's cloak lasts until your next turn.
- The class bonus counts the 3 armor pieces of one legendary set plus the weapon in hand.

The owner asked for:
- one legendary weapon for every class (today the Witch only gets the Healer's healing staff)
- legendaries that are better than epics
- set bonuses on epics and legendaries only
- more bonuses the rarer the item, with legendary bonuses that are unique to each class rather than just bigger numbers
- new pictures, 3 options per legendary (see the two picture sheets)

Nothing below is built yet. Every number can be tuned later by the balance autoplayer.

## What the game does today
- **8 legendary weapons, one per weapon type, not per class.** Classes that share a weapon type share the legendary: the Healer and Witch (staff), the Knight and Dragon Knight (lance).
- **Legendary armor:** 3 legendary armor sets, by weight (heavy Aegis of Dawn, medium Stormweave, light Nightbloom), with bonuses for wearing 2 or 3 pieces. There are also 2 legendary charms.
- **Epics have no set bonuses today.** Each epic has one special "epic bonus" (the word in its name, like *Vampiric* or *Bulwark*) plus 2–3 ordinary bonuses. That is often more than a legendary armor piece's 2–3 fixed bonuses, which is why epics can feel better.
- **Number of bonuses today:**

  | Rarity | Bonuses |
  |---|---|
  | Common | 0–1 |
  | Uncommon | 1–2 |
  | Rare | 2–3 |
  | Epic | 3–4 |
  | Legendary weapon | 4 |
  | Legendary armor | 2–3 |

## 1. Number of bonuses goes up with rarity
| Rarity | Bonuses | What kind |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 1 | ordinary |
| Uncommon | 2 | ordinary |
| Rare | 3 | ordinary |
| Epic | 4 | 1 epic bonus + 3 ordinary, plus its set bonus |
| Legendary | 5 | 2 unique class powers + 3 ordinary at the legendary (highest) numbers, plus its set bonus |

The counts are exact, not a range, so a rarer item always has more.

## 2. Set bonuses: epics and legendaries only
**Epic sets, by armor weight.** The purple armor already comes in three weights. Wearing 2 or 3 pieces of the same weight gives a bonus:
- **Vigil** (heavy: Vigil Greathelm, Cathedral Plate, Rampart Sabatons)
  - 2 pieces: 5% less damage taken
  - 3 pieces: +10% max HP and 10% thorns
- **Wolfsteel** (medium: Wolfsteel Coif, Brigandine, Pathfinder Greaves)
  - 2 pieces: +5% crit
  - 3 pieces: +8% damage and +1 move
- **Shadowweave** (light: Silk Cowl, Shadowweave Jerkin, Moonstep Boots)
  - 2 pieces: +5% evade
  - 3 pieces: +8% damage, and regen 2 HP a turn

**Legendary sets: 4 pieces, ending in a class bonus.** Each legendary set is the class's legendary weapon plus the 3 legendary armor pieces of a weight the class can wear. Its bonuses are bigger than the epic ones at 2 and 3 pieces, and wearing all 4 adds a bonus unique to the class:

| Pieces | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 2 | about double the epic 2-piece |
| 3 | about double the epic 3-piece |
| 4 (needs the weapon) | a **class bonus** (below) |

Old legendary weapons that don't fit the new list leave the loot table. Gear you already own keeps working.

## 3. One legendary weapon per class, with 2 unique powers each
Every legendary weapon can only be used by its class. The names below are the A options; the name follows the picture the owner picks.

| Class | Weapon | Unique power 1 | Unique power 2 | 4-piece class bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Warrior | sword | **Battle Rhythm:** each hit you land gives +8% damage until your next turn (up to +24%) | **Unbowed:** below 30% HP you take 25% less damage | kills reset your class ability's cooldown |
| Tank | axe | **Bulwark Aura:** allies next to you take 15% less damage | **Retaliate:** when hit, 30% chance to stun the attacker for 1 turn | your counterattacks deal +50% |
| Knight | lance | **Charge:** +5% damage for every tile moved this turn (up to +30%) | **Valor:** +1 move | the first hit you take each battle does nothing |
| Dragon Knight | lance | **Dragonblood:** 15% of your damage is added as fire, and hits burn for 2 turns | **Wyrm's Hide:** you can't be burned or frozen | your breath and dive skills hit everyone next to the target too |
| Ranger | bow | **Eagle Eye:** +1 range | **Pinning Shot:** 25% chance a hit roots the target for 1 turn | +20% damage to targets 4 or more tiles away |
| Gunslinger | gun | **Quick Hands:** once per turn, your first attack doesn't end your turn (you can still move or attack again) | **Ricochet:** 20% of your damage bounces to another enemy within 2 tiles | crits refund 1 turn of every cooldown |
| Assassin | dagger | **Ambush:** +30% damage to an enemy at full HP | **Shadowstep:** after a kill you are cloaked for 1 turn | your first attack each battle always crits |
| Mage | wand | **Arcane Echo:** 20% chance a spell casts twice | **Overflow:** −1 turn on every cooldown | your area spells are 1 tile wider |
| Healer | staff | **Sanctuary:** your heals also give a shield of 20% of the ally's max HP | **Lifeline:** once per battle, an ally who would fall stays at 1 HP | your heals also remove one bad status |
| Witch | staff | **Hex Mastery:** the statuses you cause last 1 turn longer | **Soul Siphon:** each status you cause heals you 5% of your max HP | your Voodoo Link and hexes also spread to one enemy next to the target |

Each weapon also gets 3 ordinary bonuses that suit its class (for example the Mage: fire, crit damage, damage), at the legendary numbers.

**Legendary charms:** the 2 legendary charms stay (Heart of the Stair, Phoenix Feather). They each get a 3rd bonus so they also have more bonuses than an epic charm.
