# Crusader Tactics: the Purple Tier

**Approved in full by the owner on 2026-10-11**, with these answers: power 6 stays; Executioner's and Warding go in from the start; gold chests give 65% rare, 30% purple and 5% legendary (legendary only once a hero is Lv 30, purple before that). One clarification while building: purple boots keep the +1 move that rare boots already give; there is no extra move bonus on top.

For approval, item by item. Text only; art comes with your new sprites. Each item has a number (P1, P2…). Reply "approve all", or list the numbers you want changed or cut.

## 1. What purple is for

Today the tiers jump from rare (blue, power 4) straight to legendary (gold, power 10). Since your 2026-10-11 decision, legendaries wait until a hero reaches Lv 30, which leaves the middle of the game (about Lv 18 to 30) with nothing exciting to chase. Purple (epic) fills that gap.

- **Order of tiers:** common (white), uncommon (green), rare (blue), **epic (purple)**, legendary (gold).
- **Strength:** power 6, between rare (4) and legendary (10). Legendaries stay clearly the best.
- **Who can use it:** purple weapons need weapon rank **A**. A hero's main weapon reaches rank A at Lv 18, its other weapons at Lv 20. Purple armor and charms have no rank and any class that wears that weight can use them.
- **What makes it purple:** every purple item has one **purple bonus** that only purple items roll. That bonus is the item's prefix and its name, for example "Vampiric Gravewarden Blade". On top of that it rolls 2 or 3 ordinary bonuses, a little stronger than a rare item's.
- **Colour:** #b46cf0 (purple).

| Number | Rule |
|---|---|
| P1 | Add the epic (purple) tier between rare and legendary, power 6 |
| P2 | Purple weapons need weapon rank A (Lv 18 main weapon, Lv 20 others) |
| P3 | Each purple item rolls one purple bonus (its prefix and name) plus 2 or 3 ordinary bonuses |

## 3. Where purple drops

Floors and hero levels roughly go together; these follow the floor, as all loot does today.

| Floors | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| 1-10 | 70% common, 30% uncommon | unchanged |
| 11-20 | 70% uncommon, 30% rare | unchanged |
| 21-30 | 70% uncommon, 30% rare | 50% uncommon, 40% rare, 10% purple |
| 31-60 | 93% rare, 7% legendary | 55% rare, 40% purple, 5% legendary |
| 61+ | 75% rare, 25% legendary | 30% rare, 50% purple, 20% legendary |

- Legendary still only drops once a hero is Lv 30; until then a legendary roll gives **purple** instead of rare.
- Elites still have a 30% chance of one tier better, so a rare-tier elite can drop purple.
- A boss that would give a legendary before anyone is Lv 30 gives a purple item instead.
- Chests: wood common/uncommon (unchanged), silver uncommon/rare (unchanged), **gold rare 70% / purple 30%** (today gold gives a legendary 30% of the time; legendaries now come from bosses and late floors).
- A weapon roll above the hero's rank comes down a tier until it fits, as today, so a Lv 15 hero never gets a purple weapon it can't hold.

| Number | Rule |
|---|---|
| P5 | The drop table above for floors 21+ |
| P6 | Before any hero is Lv 30, a legendary roll gives purple (not rare) |
| P7 | Gold chests: 70% rare, 30% purple |
