# Skill plan (proposal, waiting for owner approval)

Sent as a PDF on 2026-10-10. Schedule for every class: Lv 1 two normals + one ultimate; a normal at 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 27; ultimate at 20; ascension ultimate at 30. 53 new hero skills plus the Healer's new Lv 20 ultimate Last Light.

Rule changes in the proposal: Execution and Fortress Slam become normal; Resurrection becomes a normal skill at Lv 24 (still once per battle); Mage Thunder moves to Lv 3; most old skills arrive later; monsters use the same schedule (4 newest normals + newest ultimate), bosses keep their kits.

## Warrior

- Lv 1 **Slash**: A quick sword cut. Power 1.0.
- Lv 1 **Rend**: A cut that bleeds for 3 turns. Power 0.8.
- Lv 1 **Power Strike** (ultimate): A heavy two-handed blow. Power 1.6, cooldown 3.
- Lv 3 **Whirlwind** (was Lv 4): Hits the target and every enemy next to it. Power 0.9, cooldown 2.
- Lv 6 **Crescent Wave** (was Lv 8): A blade of energy 2-3 tiles away. Power 1.3, cooldown 2.
- Lv 9 **Shoulder Check** NEW: Shoves the target 2 tiles back. If it slams into a wall or another unit it takes +50% and is stunned 1 turn. Power 0.9, cooldown 2.
- Lv 12 **Execution**: Finishing blow, +50% on enemies under 40% HP. Power 2.2, cooldown 4. (Was an ultimate; now a normal skill so every class has the same number of ultimates.)
- Lv 15 **Riposte Stance** NEW: Until your next turn, strike back at every enemy that hits you in melee (power 0.8). Cooldown 4.
- Lv 18 **Leaping Cleave** NEW: Leap up to 3 tiles to land next to the target, then hit it and every enemy next to you. Power 1.1, cooldown 3.
- Lv 20 **Dragon Slash** (ultimate): A blazing fire slash with a 4-turn burn. Power 3.0, cooldown 5.
- Lv 21 **Sunder** NEW: Strips every buff and shield from the target. Power 1.2, cooldown 3.
- Lv 24 **Battle Trance** NEW: For 2 turns: +25% damage and +1 Move, but you take 15% more damage. Cooldown 4.
- Lv 27 **Momentum** NEW: Power 1.0. If it defeats the target you may act once more this turn (once per turn). Cooldown 3.

## Tank

- Lv 1 **Bash**: A solid shield-and-axe hit. Power 1.1.
- Lv 1 **Shield Bash**: Stuns the target 1 turn. Power 0.9, cooldown 3.
- Lv 1 **Crushing Blow** (ultimate): Smashes armor: -30% DEF for 2 turns. Power 1.8, cooldown 3.
- Lv 3 **Ground Pound** (was Lv 4): Hits the target and its neighbors and snares them. Power 0.8, cooldown 2.
- Lv 6 **Ironhide** (was Lv 8): Brace: take 40% less damage until your next turn ends. Cooldown 4.
- Lv 9 **Hook Chain** NEW: Throw a chain 2-3 tiles and drag the target to the tile next to you. Power 0.7, cooldown 2.
- Lv 12 **Bodyguard** NEW: Pick an ally within 3 tiles. For 2 turns, hits aimed at them hit you instead, for 70% of the damage. Cooldown 4.
- Lv 15 **Fortress Slam** (was Lv 14): Shockwave that stuns everything within 2 tiles of the target. Power 1.2, cooldown 4. (Was an ultimate; now normal.)
- Lv 18 **Iron Shell** NEW: Gain a shield worth 25% of your max HP. Cooldown 4.
- Lv 20 **Titan's Wrath** (ultimate): Golden smash that stuns the target and its neighbors. Power 2.6, cooldown 5.
- Lv 21 **Taunting Roar** NEW: Every enemy within 3 tiles must attack you on its next turn. Cooldown 4.
- Lv 24 **Aftershock** NEW: Hits the target and its neighbors and snares them; at the start of the next enemy turn the ground shakes again for half damage. Power 1.0, cooldown 3.
- Lv 27 **Unstoppable** NEW: Bulldoze up to 4 tiles in a straight line. Every enemy in the way is hit (power 0.9) and shoved to the side. Cooldown 4.

## Mage

- Lv 1 **Fireball**: Fire magic, ignores half of armor. Power 1.2.
- Lv 1 **Frost Bolt**: Ice magic that snares 1 turn. Power 1.0.
- Lv 1 **Meteor** (ultimate): A flaming rock on an area. Power 1.5, cooldown 4.
- Lv 3 **Thunder** (was Lv 1): Thunder magic, ignores half of armor. Power 1.2. (Moves from Lv 1 to Lv 3: every class starts with exactly 2 normal skills.)
- Lv 6 **Ice Lance** (was Lv 3): A long-range spear of ice. Power 1.3.
- Lv 9 **Chain Lightning** (was Lv 6): Jumps to every enemy next to the target. Power 0.9, cooldown 2.
- Lv 12 **Blizzard** (was Lv 10): Snowstorm on an area that snares everything hit. Power 1.1, cooldown 3.
- Lv 15 **Blink** NEW: Teleport to an open tile up to 4 away. You can still attack this turn. Cooldown 4.
- Lv 18 **Mana Burn** NEW: Arcane hit that adds 1 turn to every cooldown the target has. Power 0.8, cooldown 3.
- Lv 20 **Arcane Nova** (ultimate): Pure arcane blast within 2 tiles. Power 2.0, cooldown 4.
- Lv 21 **Static Field** NEW: Lightning within 2 tiles of the target; those tiles stay charged for 2 turns and shock any enemy that ends its turn on them. Power 0.6, cooldown 3.
- Lv 24 **Gravity Well** NEW: Arcane hit on an area that pulls every enemy within 2 tiles one step toward the center. Power 0.7, cooldown 3.
- Lv 27 **Echo** NEW: Cast the last spell you used again at 60% power on a new target, without starting its cooldown. Cooldown 4.

## Healer

- Lv 1 **Heal Ally**: Heals one ally up to 3 tiles away. Power 1.2.
- Lv 1 **Mend Self**: Heals yourself. Power 0.8.
- Lv 1 **Divine Grace** (ultimate): Huge heal anywhere on the map, but you can't move for 2 turns. Power 3.0.
- Lv 3 **Radiant Burst**: Holy light damage, strong against dark. Power 1.0.
- Lv 6 **Blessing** (was Lv 4): You and allies within 4 deal 25% more damage for 2 turns. Cooldown 3.
- Lv 9 **Cleanse** (was Lv 6): Removes bleed, snare, stun and armor break.
- Lv 12 **Holy Siphon** (was Lv 8): Light damage, then heals your most hurt ally for all of it. Power 0.9, cooldown 2.
- Lv 15 **Mass Heal** (was Lv 10): Heals the target and every ally within 2 tiles. Power 1.0, cooldown 3.
- Lv 18 **Prism Aura** (was Lv 12): You and allies within 4 ignore enemy typing for 2 turns. Cooldown 5.
- Lv 20 **Last Light** (ultimate) NEW: NEW ULTIMATE. You and allies within 3 tiles are healed 30% and, for 2 turns, a blow that would defeat them leaves them at 1 HP instead. Cooldown 6.
- Lv 21 **Sacred Flame** (was Lv 14): Holy fire on the target and enemies next to it. Power 1.1, cooldown 2.
- Lv 24 **Resurrection** (was Lv 16): Revives the most recently fallen ally next to you at 40% HP, once per battle. (Was the ultimate; now a normal skill.)
- Lv 27 **Renewal** (was Lv 18): You and allies within 4 regenerate 12% HP each turn for 3 turns. Cooldown 4.
