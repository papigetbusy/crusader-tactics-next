# Art rule

Locked 2026-10-12 on Blood Frenzy. That sheet and that review GIF are the standard. Do not loosen this.

One skill, one prompt, one sheet. Batches of about six. An ascension batch is the three skills and the three class abilities, each on its own body.

Call the hero with @ and the saved name. Do not describe the face, clothes, weapon, or colors. The saved picture is the design.

Eight frames. Not nine. Eight equal cells. Nine makes a cell get skipped.

Two rows. Row 1 is that one hero, full body, no spell. Row 2 is the spell only, no hero. Spell out all eight body lines and all eight effect lines. Do not collapse the effects into one sentence. Name the pose. Name the color. Body 5 and body 6 are the same pose, held. Body 8 is an exact copy of body 1. Effect 8 is almost gone.

The label under each cell is the exact text from the prompt, and it does not touch the picture. Background is flat #FF00FF. Do not write a color code on the sheet. No floor, no scenery, no enemy.

An ascension move uses that ascension. Blood Frenzy is @Berserker, not @Warrior. The "do not draw" line names the other bodies and the gear they carry.

Where the effect sits is decided per skill, after the sheet is in:

- A power-up stays on him, at his feet, behind him. Blood Frenzy. Oath of Protection.
- A hit travels to the enemy. Blood Rage. Holy Judgment. The effect still must not cover his face.

The review then plays the eight bodies, draws the sheet's own effect, and ends on body 1 three times with the glow gone. He does not stick on the attack pose. Nothing goes into the game until you say that GIF is the one.

```
Sprite sheet for FILL SKILL. One character only: @FILL HERO.
Use the saved design. Do not change the face, clothes, weapon, colors, or size.
Do not draw FILL NOT.
Flat background #FF00FF. Do not write a color code on the sheet. No floor, no scenery, no enemy.

Two rows. Eight equal cells. Label under each picture, exact text, not touching the picture.

Row 1, @FILL HERO only. Full body in every cell. No spell. No second character.
FILL SKILL BODY 1 — idle.
FILL SKILL BODY 2 — the start of the move.
FILL SKILL BODY 3 — the windup.
FILL SKILL BODY 4 — reaching into the move.
FILL SKILL BODY 5 — the move, held. He is still fully visible.
FILL SKILL BODY 6 — the same pose, still held. He is still fully visible.
FILL SKILL BODY 7 — coming back to idle.
FILL SKILL BODY 8 — exact copy of body 1.

Row 2, the spell only. No character.
FILL SKILL EFFECT 1 — the first small piece. Name the color.
FILL SKILL EFFECT 2 — the next step.
FILL SKILL EFFECT 3 — bigger.
FILL SKILL EFFECT 4 — the full shape. Name the dark part and the bright edge.
FILL SKILL EFFECT 5 — the same shape, a little larger.
FILL SKILL EFFECT 6 — held.
FILL SKILL EFFECT 7 — breaking apart.
FILL SKILL EFFECT 8 — almost gone.
```
