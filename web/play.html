Version 0.22.2 “Vigil”: what’s new
  • 0.22.2: new Settings → Saves tab: copy or download your save, and import a save from a file or pasted code into any slot (it asks before replacing a slot). Balance is unchanged.
  • 0.22.1 balance: tuned with a new skilled autoplayer that uses class abilities, avoids danger, focuses kills and uses terrain (it was winning 90%+ of fights and reaching floor 120+). Floors now get harder faster (+0.7 monster levels per floor, plus an extra +0.4 per floor after floor 30), every hero beyond 5 adds one more monster to each floor, and big monster groups lose less strength per extra monster. 40% of each kill’s XP is now shared among the rest of the party so healers and supports keep up. Potions stay at 3 per hero per floor.
  • 0.22.0 Vigil: new Settings screen (☰ → Settings) with Options, How to Play (plus a link to the Codex), What’s New and a copy-able bug Report. Map controls redesigned: zoom slider, Flat/Isometric switch, minimap and danger-zone switches, battle speed (1×, 2×, Instant) and Rotate / Next hero buttons. Danger zone shades every tile monsters can reach. After a hero acts the camera moves to the next ready hero; End Turn glows when everyone has acted (optional auto end) and asks before ending if heroes haven’t acted. Skip button during the enemy turn. Floor-clear summary (XP, level-ups, loot, gold). Floor list to travel between floors, showing cleared floors, chests left and boss loot. Autosave at the start of every turn and resume mid-battle. Gear sheet streamlined: CAPPED tag, bag sorting, stat comparison against worn gear, and a Drop button with confirmation. First-time tips. Options for damage numbers, reduced flashing, low graphics and colorblind-friendly tiles. Units only turn into map markers when zoomed far out.
  • 0.21.0 Keep: floors are now fixed for each run: going back to a floor you already reached gives the exact same map, terrain and monsters. Chests you opened stay opened, shops keep the same stock (bought items stay sold), and companion spirits do not reappear. Replaying a cleared floor gives half gold and full XP. Bosses that already dropped a legendary fall back to that floor’s normal loot chances. Level cap: heroes can be at most 5 levels above the monsters on your highest floor; a notice explains it, and capped heroes earn no XP until you clear that floor. Isometric view now uses the same Tiny Swords art as the flat view (ground, coasts, water, bridges, roads, cliffs, trees, houses, castle).
  • 0.20.1: the attack forecast is now a small red window that floats beside the monster you are targeting (so you can see what you are about to hit), with the same info: target HP before → after (KO tag when it will die), DEF, damage, hit %, WEAK/RESIST, skill and weapon, status effects, your HP, target switch, weapon swap, cancel and attack. Heals and buffs use a green window. Top-corner windows now stay below the phone or app header.
  • 0.20.0 Nightfall: new gothic look: crimson and gold UI, night-graded battlefield with vignette, Grenze Gotisch + EB Garamond fonts; Tiny Swords map art (Pixel Frog), Raven Fantasy Icons for gear (Clockwork Raven), and unique layered attack animations for every skill, monster attack and class ability (Super Pixel Effects Gigapack by Will Tice / unTied Games, plus Tiny Swords effects). Classic Fire Emblem controls: tap a hero, tap a blue tile to move (or tap the hero again to act in place), then pick from a command menu beside them; skills fly out to the side; aiming shows a two-sided combat forecast; tap any empty tile for the system menu (End Turn, Gear, Save, map tools, floors). The map uses the whole screen: status badges only show on the unit you select or target, and tapping a monster opens its info window. The Chocobo is gone, replaced by the Carrion Strider.
  • 0.19.2: while aiming an attack or ability, the map buttons are hidden (they were showing up as stretched bars); only the target list and the attack bar remain. Cancel brings the normal controls back. Weapon-set buttons only show when the hero actually has a weapon equipped. Pinch, drag and the mouse wheel still zoom and pan while aiming.
  • 0.19.1: new ◇/▦ button in the map controls switches between flat and isometric view instantly; the 🗺 minimap button lights up when the minimap is on and turns it off with one tap; the minimap shows your view box in isometric too.
  • 0.19.0 Frontier: big generated battlefields (14×14 growing to 32×32) with mountain ridges and passes, rivers and bridges, forests (slow, −15% damage taken), hills, roads (fast), forts (−25% damage, heal 10%/turn); more enemies (up to 16); spawns at opposite ends; Seize-the-throne floors and reinforcement floors. New camera: drag anywhere to pan, scroll wheel or pinch zooms where you point, WASD/arrow keys, minimap you can tap, Next Hero button, fast-forward enemy turns, simple markers when zoomed far out, and an optional auto-zoom setting.
  • 0.18.0 Ascendant: every class ability evolves at Lv 5 and Lv 20, and at Lv 30 each ascension has its own version (one Pure path that keeps everything, two new playstyles): 30 abilities in all, including Grave Bloom skeleton armies, Blood Pact, traps, banners, fire fields, clones, Overwatch and Sky Lord. Ascension can be changed once per floor from the Gear sheet. Gear affixes now match the class they drop for (no healing bonus on guns).
  • 0.17.0 Valor: every class gets a class ability (gold button, free action, has its own cooldown): Double Strike, Iron Maiden, Inner Element, Enlightenment, Hunter’s Eye, Bloodlust, Quick Draw, Beast Mode, Poison Ivy, Vow of Honor. Both weapon slots now take any weapon your class can use (carry a fire weapon and a crit weapon and swap per attack). Assassin now uses daggers only and dual-wields them like the Gunslinger, plus a new base skill, Cloak. New statuses: Burn and Ivy.
  • 0.16.2: the camera no longer zooms in on its own when you select a hero or a skill; zoom is fully up to you (it still follows your selection if you're zoomed in). The flat Fire Emblem view is now the main build.
  • 0.16.1: zoom and rotate buttons moved into the bottom panel (and the attack bar) so they never cover your units; buttons ignore iPhone double-tap zoom; pinch with two fingers to zoom, mouse wheel on computers.
  • 0.16.0 Flat: new top-down 2D map view like Fire Emblem (square tiles, camera straight above, raised ground shown with a cliff edge). Everything else plays the same. The old isometric view is still available: Gear → Map view.
  • 0.15.0 Touch: attacking hides the menus and shows floating windows: a numbered target list at the top (health bars, damage, hit and crit chance) and a compact bar at the bottom (weapon sets, Cancel, Attack); every skill is tap-target-then-confirm; the camera zooms to fit you and your targets; bigger tiles and sprites on phones; the ascension choice fits small screens, plus a Choose Ascension button in Gear as a backup; damage math fixes: dodge and Phantom count in the shown hit %, element affixes apply to element-typed attacks, physical affixes no longer boost staff/wand strikes, enemy buffs like Dark Pact now work, staffs and wands boost healing.
  • 0.14.1: companion spirits now glow and pulse blue with a “Companion Spirit” label; bonded companions and Necromancer summons keep their monster sprite (they were showing a hero sprite).
  • 0.14.0 Armory: Fire Emblem GBA-style weapon ranks (E–S) per class, 2 weapon sets (Dragon Knight 3, Gunslinger dual guns), weapon rank needed by rarity (legendary = S), hit chance on every attack with ±15% weapon triangle, Light/Medium/Heavy armor by class, new guns, wands and daggers, 19 unique legendaries including 3 armor sets with set bonuses, smarter loot that targets gear your party can use.
  • 0.13.1: affix count now depends on rarity: common 0–1, uncommon 1–2, rare 2–3, legendary 3–4.
  • 0.13.0 Arcana: smooth tile-by-tile movement with no ground clipping; status badges above heads (value, stat, turns) with colored glows; Shock from thunder roots targets; all ranged attacks use range 2–6; attack-range tiles after moving; tap a monster to see its move/attack zones, weaknesses and skills; 7 random biomes with pixel textures; wood, silver and gold chests; loot rarity scales with floor; random gear affixes (PoE-style) that work in combat, and combining mixes and amplifies them; unique animations for every skill, bigger for higher-level skills, plus caster poses (gun recoil, potion throws, casting circles, knight charges).
  • 0.12.1: hero pick and save-file screens fit iPhone screens in portrait; the Begin and Back buttons always stay on screen and the list scrolls.
  • 0.12.0 Oath: the Witch Knight is now two classes. The Witch casts poison and curses (new Poison and Weak statuses) and ascends to Necromancer, Blood Witch or Hex Queen. The Knight rides 7 tiles, charges for +12% damage per tile moved, and ascends to Templar, Black Knight or Storm Rider. Both use your new sprites and new evolution art.
  • 0.11.0 Crusade: title screen (pixel castle, candlelit “Crusader Tactics” logo), New Game with a 5-hero pick from all 9 classes, Continue with 3 save files, Save button in battle, autosave to your current file.
  • 0.10.0 Legion: 4 new hero classes (Assassin, Gunslinger, Witch Knight, Dragon Knight) with evolutions and 12 new ascensions, including the Necromancer who raises the monsters she kills; 6 new monsters, 5 new elites, 3 new bosses; rare companion spirits (blue glow) that bond with the hero who defeats them; fixed the swapped Shadow Assassin and Orc Brute sprites.
  • 0.9.0 Frontier: attack damage back to 0.7 values; maps grow from 10×10 to 20×20; more enemies every 5 floors (up to 12); max 1 enemy healer, and enemy healers can attack; bosses every 10 floors with 25% legendary drops; gold, shops every 7 floors (buy, sell, combine); item drop icons; zoom + drag camera; a new hero every 25 floors (up to 8); a free legendary every 100 floors.
  • 0.8.0 Metamorphosis: evolutions change the sprite (capes, armor, crowns, wings, masks) and glow a new color at each stage; more damage overall; skills level up to Lv 10 (+12% per level) and switching skills counts double toward mastery; XP only goes to the hero who landed the hit; 6 new Healer skills with team buffs (Blessing, Prism Aura, Renewal) and attack-heal hybrids; bleed scales with max HP.
  • 0.7.0 Ascension: 4 skill slots + 1 ultimate, 22 new hero skills, 10 monster skills, evolutions at Lv 5 and 20, 15 specializations at Lv 30, skill levels, damage forecasts, weakness info, equipped weapon sets your weapon type, ±10% damage variance.
  • 0.6 Endless: infinite floors scaled to your party, balance pass, saving, offline build, undo move, rotating camera.
  • 0.5 Arsenal: 20 weapons, elites and bosses, 10x10 map.
  • 0.4 Spark: Fire Emblem-style attack effects, HP bars, damage numbers, new UI.
  • 0.3 Gear: character sheet, gear slots, potions.
  • 0.2 Sprites: your sprite sheet, FFT-style UI.
  • 0.1 First Floor: classes, triangles, floors.
